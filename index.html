ABOUT THE PRAVACHAN

The Crown Jewel of All Scriptures

Composed by Veda Vyasa — the essence of all Brahma Sutras & Upanishads, revered as the Fifth Veda

Importance of Shrimad Bhagwat Mahapuran

Shrimad Bhagwatam by Veda Vyasa is considered the crown jewel among all scriptures. As declared in the Garuda Purana: “arthō'ya˙m brahma-sūtrā˙nām… śrīmad-bhāgavatābhidha˙h” — Shrimad Bhagwatam encapsulates the essence of all Brahma Sutras and Upanishads. It is the Panchama Veda — the Fifth Veda.

Veda Vyasa declares: nimna-gānā˙m yathā ga&ndot;gā / devānām acyuto yathā / vaiš˙navānā˙m yathā śambhu˙h / purā˙nānām ida˙m tathā — Just as the Ganga is greatest among rivers, Lord Acyuta supreme among deities, and Lord Sambhu greatest of Vaishnavas, so Shrimad Bhagwatam is the greatest of all Puranas.

Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji, one of India's most prominent spiritual leaders, will reveal the secrets of this Shrimad Bhagwatam in Udaipur in a 9-day discourse from 17th to 25th May.

Pujaniya

"भक्ति की शीतल किरण पूँज" — The Soothing Rays of Bhakti

Pujaniya Raseshwari Devi Ji is one of India's most prominent spiritual leaders and a senior preacher of Bhaktiyog Rasavatar Jagadguru Swami Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj — the greatest authority on devotion in this age, who emphatically taught that without bhakti, all paths of karma, jñāna and yoga become fruitless.

From childhood itself, Shri Maharaj Ji blessed her with his divine association, nurtured her heart with the nectar of bhakti, and moulded her into a Mahabhagavat personality, dedicating her entirely to the welfare of the world. If Shri Maharaj Ji is the sun of bhakti, Pujaniya Devi Ji is its soothing, cooling rays.

For over three decades, Pujaniya Devi Ji has been propagating Shri Krishna bhakti across India and abroad. Her Bhagwat discourse is not an ordinary recitation — it is a soul-level offering made before the Lord, born of compassion for souls afflicted by the impurities of Kali Yuga, aimed at immersing weary hearts in the colour of divine love for Shri Krishna.

WHY LISTEN

Why Should One Listen to this Bhagavat

“श्रवणं सर्वधर्मेभ्यो वरं मन्ये तपोधनाः।॥” — Narada Ji declares: listening to the Bhagavat is superior to all other forms of dharma.

In this age, Jagadguru Swami Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj, the greatest authority on devotion, repeatedly emphasised that without bhakti, all paths — karma, jñāna and yoga — become fruitless.

Shrimad Bhagavat Mahapurana, being the divine voice of God and illuminated with the devotional lives of the greatest saints, infuses the Nectar of Bhakti into the heart of the listener. It is the life-restoring elixir for seekers and instantly draws the soul closer to the Supreme Lord.

When the divinity of the speaker, the austerity and surrender of the yajman, and the pure devotion of the listeners come together, the entire atmosphere becomes charged with spiritual power.

(Bhāg. 1.2.29) — Vasudeva is the supreme knowledge, supreme austerity, supreme dharma and supreme refuge for all beings.

WHAT MAKES THIS UNIQUE

In What Way This Bhāgavat Is Unique

This is not an ordinary recitation — it is a supreme act of benevolence offered by an exceedingly gracious heart for the welfare of all souls

The Śrīmad Bhāgavatam is neither a historical chronicle nor a collection of mythological episodes; it is the nectar-filled, living form of devotion in scripture — revealed solely to infuse the parched hearts of beings in the age of Kali with the divine essence of bhakti.

Yet, it is often seen that traditional Bhāgavat kathā events become confined to ritualistic formalities or reduced to mere entertainment, thereby failing to fulfil their sacred purpose.

The inspiration behind this particular event is the heartfelt desire to spread Śrī Kṛṣṇa-bhakti across India and abroad. This kathā is an intimate, soul-level offering made before the Lord by a saintly heart overflowing with compassion for beings afflicted by the impurities of Kali Yuga.

GETTING THERE

Your Journey to Udaipur

The City of Lakes welcomes you. Here's how to reach the sacred venue.

✈️

By Air

Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) is well connected from Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur and Kolkata. The venue is ~25 min from the airport in central Udaipur.

🚂

By Train

Udaipur City Railway Station connects to major cities: Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Delhi, Ajmer, Udaipur Express (Delhi) and Mewar Express are popular routes.

🚌

By Road

Udaipur is connected via NH-48 from Ahmedabad (~4 hrs), Jaipur (~6 hrs) and Mumbai (~11 hrs). RSRTC and private buses available.
